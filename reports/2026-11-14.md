> As modern NVMe SSDs become increasingly capable of in-device computation, workloads such as SQL filtering and key-value store operations are now being offloaded from the host to the SSD. These tasks often involve small payloads, typically a few dozen to hundreds of bytes, which are inefficiently processed by the standard NVMe interface due to its fixed memory page-granularity DMA transfer. Regardless of how small the payload is, even a few dozen bytes, each request still incurs memory page-sized PCIe traffic, resulting in significant bandwidth waste and latency overhead.
